Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Policy explains how Lei Huang, operating under the ZINC brand (“ZINC”, “we”, “us” or “our”), collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase or use our services, or contact support.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information, including email address, account identifiers and authentication records.
  • Order and transaction information, including plan, amount, currency, payment status, order number and tax or billing details supplied at checkout. We do not store full payment-card details.
  • Service and technical data, including IP address, device and browser information, login records, traffic usage totals, connection timestamps, selected server or region, and security or diagnostic logs.
  • Support communications, including ticket content and information you choose to provide.
  • Cookies and similar local-storage data required for sessions, preferences, security and basic analytics.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, deliver purchased services, process orders, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain and improve reliability, comply with tax and legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, and communicate essential service or account notices.

3. Legal grounds

We process information as necessary to perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations, protect our legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service, and, where required, based on your consent. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We disclose only what is necessary to providers that support hosting, network delivery, security, email, customer support, analytics, tax and payment processing. Waffo Pancake and other payment providers process checkout and payment information under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and infrastructure, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. International processing

Network, hosting and payment providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use contractual or other legally recognized safeguards for cross-border transfers.

6. Retention

We keep account and transaction records while your account is active and for the period required by applicable tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal rules. Operational and security logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, abuse prevention and network security. Support records are retained while needed to resolve requests and document outcomes. Information is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

7. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, credential protection, monitoring and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure; please use a unique password and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized account access.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. You may also close your account and complain to the competent data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request, and some records must be retained where the law requires.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies or local storage for login sessions, security and language or interface preferences. Optional analytics, if enabled, are used to understand aggregate website performance. Browser controls can remove or block cookies, but blocking essential storage may prevent account features from working.

10. Children

The service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

11. Policy changes

We may update this Policy to reflect legal, technical or service changes. We will update the effective date and provide notice of material changes through the website or account communications.
